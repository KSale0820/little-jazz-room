# Pixel plant growth stages (0–4) — new review assets

Five isolated transparent PNG sprites, based on the five-stage pixel plant sheet approved in chat.

- `plant-stage-0.png`: soil-filled terracotta pot, no sprout
- `plant-stage-1.png`: small two-leaf sprout
- `plant-stage-2.png`: young plant with new top growth
- `plant-stage-3.png`: established leafy plant
- `plant-stage-4.png`: mature flowering plant with two ivory blooms

Each uses the same 224 × 362 pixel transparent canvas. The pot baseline and horizontal alignment are consistent throughout the sequence. The sheet was split at transparent column gaps, downscaled 2:1 with nearest-neighbor sampling and exported to a compact indexed palette, without blurred edges or a black background.

New review assets only: existing original images and their paths remain untouched. No app code or manifest integration. PR #84 stays a draft until approved.
