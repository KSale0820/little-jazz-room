# Design decisions — authoritative summary

**The source of truth for the approved visual is `FINAL-DESIGN-LOCK.md` and `APPROVED-VISUAL-REFERENCE.png`.** Historical images/decisions are background, not directions for the app to revert to older layouts.

- Light ivory/sage UI throughout, including Practice; high contrast black notation. Less busy than initial mockups.
- Home large, illustrated, tappable jazz room with pianist/bass/sax/trumpet/guitar/drums/record player, botanicals and day-to-night transitions. Standard navigation still accessible. Preserve daily goal, gentle streak, Today's Practice, large Start, Quick Access and long-term goal.
- Explore searchable routines and exercises, categories AND list; no category icons, no icons in exercise rows.
- Create routine/editor clear and simple; custom exercises work without song/recording; share/remix essential.
- Practice Chords / Player switch, measure grid not guitar fret chart. Optional bottom cat companionship as approved, but Focus Mode removes it.
- Chosen cat same on Home/Profile, eight original anatomically sound builds, subtle idle; roles: piano, sax, bass, drums, trumpet, guitar, vocals, turntable. Milo sunglasses, Pepper neckerchief, Biscuit headphones. Props must not add limbs or tails; no sprite-sheet animation unless QA.
- Real recorded audio/stems assumed long-term; no fake audio processing Sunday. Unlicensed YouTube recordings excluded. Team-only signup, public chart browsing.

Reference: `FINAL-DESIGN-LOCK.md`, `CAT-JAZZ-IDENTITIES.md`, `AUDIO-DATA-CONTRACT.md`, `MVP-LAUNCH-GATES.md`.
